## Engagement Hooks

- **"I believe enterprise architecture should be transparent."** -- a values statement, not a sales pitch. This opens the post with credibility and principle.
- **Recap bullet list** rewards the audience that followed the full series. It also functions as a standalone summary for new viewers who discover the post organically.
- **"This is a starting point."** -- humble, non-prescriptive language. You are not selling The Answer. You are sharing a methodology. This resonates with senior architects who distrust anyone claiming to have a silver bullet.
- **Two CTAs, both outbound:** The website and GitHub. No gated content, no email capture forms. This is a trust play -- give everything away, and serious leads will reach out directly.
- **"Let's talk."** -- two words. No calendar link, no booking page, no "DM me." The people who act on this line are the highest-quality leads.
