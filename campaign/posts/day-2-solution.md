Here is the blueprint for an Idea Factory.

## Engagement Hooks

- **"This isn't shadow IT."** -- directly addresses the objection before the audience raises it. Preemptive objection handling builds credibility.
- **Carousel format** drives 2-3x more dwell time than single images on LinkedIn. The algorithm rewards document posts with higher reach.
- **Bullet list of principles** is scannable and shareable. Architects will screenshot these and share in Slack channels.
- **"Here's what I learned"** reframes experimentation from "risky" to "learning" -- psychologically safe language.
