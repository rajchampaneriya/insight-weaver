## Engagement Hooks

- **"The innovation math is broken"** -- frames the problem in business language, not technology language. This is the post that gets forwarded to the CFO.
- **Side-by-side comparison** is the most shareable format in enterprise content. It reduces a complex argument to a scannable visual proof.
- **"12 experiments in a month"** -- specific, credible metric. Not "10x improvement" (which sounds like marketing). "12 vs 1-2" is concrete and believable.
- **Tech stack mention** (.NET Aspire, IBM Carbon) serves dual purpose: signals enterprise-grade tooling and makes the post discoverable by those technology communities.
- **Cliffhanger** ("Tomorrow, I share the blueprint") primes Day 7 engagement. By now, the audience has 5 days of context -- the blueprint offer will land with weight.
